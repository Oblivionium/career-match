# Resume Analyst

Extracts a structured profile from a student or early-career resume: skills, education,
level (student / fresher / experienced) and the roles the person is best suited for.
Part of the CareerMatch multi-agent system.

**Input:** `ResumeRequest(session_id, resume_text)`  **Output:** `ResumeProfile`

Keywords: resume parser, CV parsing, skills extraction, students, career
